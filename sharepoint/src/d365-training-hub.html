Support

Questions people ask most

All FAQs
Where do I find the guide for my task?

Search at the top of this page by task, guide number or owner, or pick your business area. Each procedure guide opens as a PDF from the D365 Procedure Guides library.

Who do I ask about how a process works?

The Business Process Owner for that area. Owners are listed on each business area above and next to every procedure guide.

A procedure guide looks out of date

Report it with the D365 issue form on the User Support Hub. Include the guide number, for example PG 301, and what has changed.

Something in D365 isn't working

Raise a support request with the D365 issue form on the User Support Hub, whether it's a technical problem or a bug.

I missed a live session

Watch the training recordings whenever you need a refresher or missed a session.
